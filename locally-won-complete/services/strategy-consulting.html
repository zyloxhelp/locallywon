The Foundation of Everything

Marketing Without Strategy Is Just Expensive Noise

You've probably been there. You spent money on Google Ads. Hired someone to run Instagram. Maybe tried SEO. And yet — the phone isn't ringing the way you hoped. Enquiries are slow. You're not sure what's working and what's just burning cash.

Here's the truth most agencies won't tell you: running campaigns without a proper strategy is like driving in a new city without Google Maps. You might eventually get there, but you'll waste a lot of time, fuel, and patience along the way.

At Locally Won, Strategy & Consulting is where every client journey begins. Before we write a single ad or publish a single blog post — we study your market, understand your customer, and build a plan that makes sense for you. Not a copy-paste template. A real strategy.

What's Included

Everything We Cover in Your Strategy

A thorough, actionable strategy built around your specific business, city, and goals — not a generic framework.

01

Business & Market Discovery

We learn everything about your business — what you sell, who you serve, what problems you solve, and what makes you different from the competition in your specific city.

02

Competitor Research & Benchmarking

A thorough analysis of your top competitors — their SEO, ads, social media, and content. We find where they're strong, where they're weak, and where you can stand out.

03

Customer Persona Development

Detailed, data-backed profiles of your ideal customers — their demographics, buying behavior, pain points, and the exact language they use when searching for what you offer.

04

Channel Strategy & Prioritization

Not every business needs to be everywhere. We help you identify the right channels for your specific business and tell you how much effort to invest in each one.

05

Goal Setting & KPI Definition

We set specific, measurable goals with you. Not "we want more traffic" but "we want to go from 2,000 to 8,000 monthly visitors with a 3% conversion rate in 6 months."

06

Budget Planning & ROI Projections

We help you decide how much to spend and where to spend it — with the reasoning behind every recommendation and realistic projections of expected returns.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you'd want to know before getting started.

How long does the strategy process take? +
A full strategy engagement typically takes 2–3 weeks, depending on the complexity of your business and the depth of research required. We don't rush it — good strategy needs proper time.
Do I need an existing digital presence? +
Not at all. We work with businesses at every stage — from those just starting their digital journey to those who have been at it for years and feel like results have plateaued.
Can I just get the strategy and execute it myself? +
Absolutely. Some clients use us purely for strategy and then execute in-house or with other partners. We're happy to set you up for success and be available for questions along the way.
How is this different from just reading marketing books? +
Generic advice is a starting point. A custom strategy built specifically for your business, your market, your competitors, and your budget is a weapon. The difference shows up in your results.
Do you also help with execution after the strategy? +
Yes. Most of our strategy clients choose to continue with us for execution across SEO, paid ads, content, social media, or all of the above. But it's entirely your choice.
