We scan your website and existing reviews to understand your tone and communication style. You can also add custom instructions. Every reply mirrors your personality — formal, friendly, or anything in between.

Only if you want them to be. By default, every AI draft waits for your approval before going live. Switch to fully automatic mode anytime once you're satisfied with the quality.

Yes. Data stays in your own Google Sheet — we only write the columns we need and never read, modify, or sell your other data. Revoke our access anytime from your Sheet's Share settings.
