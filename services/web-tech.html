FAQs

Website Questions Answered

The things our clients ask us most often before starting a project.

How long does a website take to build? +
Most business websites take 3–5 weeks from start to launch. E-commerce projects take a bit longer. Landing pages can be done faster. Timeline depends on complexity and how quickly you can provide feedback.
Will I be able to update the website myself? +
Yes. We build on platforms like WordPress that are easy to manage. We provide training and documentation so you can update content, add pages, and manage your site without needing a developer for routine changes.
Is the website mobile-friendly? +
Always. We design for mobile first — because over 80% of your Indian visitors are likely browsing on their phones. Your site will look and work perfectly on every screen size.
Do you help with hosting and domain? +
Yes. We can recommend and set up reliable hosting suited to your traffic needs, or work with your existing hosting provider. We also help with domain setup and SSL certificates.
