What Does Local SEO in London Actually Involve?

Local SEO in London covers everything that shapes your visibility in searches with a place attached. Some of it lives on your website and some of it lives elsewhere on the web.

Google Business Profile

Categories, services, service areas, photos, posts and opening hours, kept accurate and active.

Reviews

A simple routine for asking happy customers, plus thoughtful replies to every review, good or bad.

Borough and service pages

A page for each service, and for each area you genuinely cover, with local detail that proves it.

Citations

The same business name, address and phone number on Yell, Bing Places, Apple Maps and trade directories.

Local links

Mentions from local press, suppliers, associations and community sites that tie you to London.

Tracking

Calls, direction requests and enquiries measured, so you know which areas are paying off.

Why Is London a Different Local Search Market?

London has 32 boroughs plus the City of London, and close to 9 million residents. For local search, it behaves less like one city and more like dozens of towns pressed together.

Your ranking changes from street to street

Google says local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. Because distance matters, a locksmith who ranks first in Camden may barely appear in Clapham. Very few businesses rank everywhere in London, and you don't need to.

People search by area name and postcode

Londoners often add an area to their search, such as "electrician Walthamstow" or "physio Canary Wharf". Some even search by postcode district. Your pages should use the names your customers actually use, not just "London".

Directories and lead platforms compete with you

For many trades, platforms like Checkatrade, Bark and Yell rank on page one. You can't always outrank them for broad terms, but you can win the map pack, which sits above them.

What this means for you: pick the boroughs where you get the most profitable jobs and win those first. Spreading thin across all of London usually means ranking nowhere.

How Do You Get a Google Business Profile Ready to Rank?

Your profile is the single biggest factor in the map pack. Most of the London profiles we audit have at least one of these gaps:

  • Wrong primary category. "Plumber" and "Plumbing supply store" show up for very different searches.

  • Address shown when it shouldn't be. If you visit customers and don't receive them at your premises, Google's guidelines say to hide your address and set service areas instead.

  • Empty services list. Adding each service with a short description helps Google match you to specific searches.

  • No recent photos or posts. Real photos of your team, van and work build trust before anyone calls.

  • A business name stuffed with keywords. It breaks Google's rules and can get the profile suspended.

Why Do Reviews Matter So Much for Local SEO in London?

Reviews are part of what Google calls prominence, and they're also what customers read before they ring. Strong local SEO in London depends on a steady stream of genuine reviews, not a one-off burst.

BrightLocal's Local Consumer Review Survey found that 97% of consumers read reviews for local businesses. In a city with this much choice, a profile with a handful of reviews from three years ago loses to one with recent, detailed feedback.

  • Ask at the moment the customer is happiest, usually right after the job is done

  • Send a direct link by text or WhatsApp, so it takes seconds

  • Reply to every review, and handle complaints calmly and publicly

  • Never buy reviews or offer discounts for them, which Google's policies prohibit

Does Local SEO Help You Show Up in AI Answers?

Yes. When someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews to recommend a business in London, those tools lean on the same signals local SEO builds: consistent business details, clear service pages, structured data and what customers say about you.

We open your key pages with a short, direct answer, mark up your business with schema, and keep your details identical everywhere. No one can guarantee an AI mention, but this makes you far easier to find and quote.

Should You Invest in Local SEO or Google Ads First?

Many London businesses ask this, and the honest answer is often both, for different reasons.

  • Google Ads can put you at the top of results tomorrow, which suits new businesses or seasonal peaks. But you pay for every click, and in competitive London trades those clicks add up fast. When the budget stops, so do the leads.

  • Local SEO takes longer to build, but the map pack and organic results don't charge per click. Once you rank, each extra enquiry costs you very little.

A sensible approach is to run ads on your most profitable services while local SEO builds, then reduce ad spend in the boroughs where you start ranking well. Your tracking will show you when that point arrives.

A Five-Minute Local SEO Self-Check

Try these now. Each "no" is a quick win.

  • Search your main service plus your borough. Are you in the top three map results?

  • Is your primary category the most specific one available?

  • Have you had a new Google review in the last month?

  • Are your name, address and phone number identical on your site, Google and Yell?

  • Does your website have a page for each service you want calls for?

Local SEO in London: Common Questions

What is the difference between local SEO and regular SEO?

Regular SEO helps your website rank across a whole country or topic. Local SEO focuses on searches with a place attached, like "boiler repair Islington", and on the Google Maps results where most local calls come from.

Can I rank in the map pack if I work from home?

Yes. Google lets service-area businesses hide their address and list the areas they cover instead. You'll usually rank best near your verified location, so we build pages and reviews around the boroughs you serve most.

How long does local SEO take in London?

Profile fixes can show changes within weeks. Ranking consistently across several boroughs usually takes a few months of steady reviews, pages and citations.

Should I pay for Checkatrade, Yell or similar directories?

Free listings are worth having for consistent details. Paid placements can bring leads in some trades, but treat them as advertising and track the return, separately from SEO.

Is it against Google's rules to buy or swap reviews?

Yes. Google's policies prohibit fake reviews and offering incentives for reviews. We help you ask real customers at the right moment instead.
